# Old Brain Show Bible v0 (2026-11-26, draft for Sara)

## 1. Faces
Tiny dot-eye faces carry little, so faces are a small fixed set, readable at phone size.
- Neutral, Shock (eyes become rings, O mouth), Panic (rings + wobble mouth), Smug (half-lid + side smirk), Rage (V brows + teeth line), Scheming (narrowed eyes + grin), Defeated (droop eyes + down mouth), Deadpan (flat lids, flat line).
- Each archetype keeps its default face (spec v1); swaps only on beats.
- Comedy rule: the funniest cut is any face to Deadpan Denier.

## 2. Body
Silhouette first; the body sells the joke.
- Core 12 poses per archetype: idle, point, recoil, run, celebrate, collapse, plead, fight, offer/sell, arms crossed, whisper, freeze.
- Every spoken line gets a pose change. Exaggerate. Hold the pose a beat before the punchline.

## 3. Environments
- One master location per era per episode, same composition both eras so the mirror reads instantly.
- Stone Age: cave mouth, firepit, ridge, river, hunt field. Modern: living room, office, street, kitchen, phone screen.
- Wall mode for intro mirror, explanations and the end reveal.

## 4. Camera motion (one move per shot, max)
Slow push-in (tension), snap zoom (punchline), whip pan (era cut), shake (panic), pull-back (end reveal onto the cave wall).

## 5. Camera angles
Wide (establish), low angle (power: Prophet, Owner), high angle (small: Panicker, Denier), face close-up (punchline), over-shoulder (confrontation), split screen (mirror).

## 6. Storylines: addictive, not educational
Formula: Hook 0-2s (the trigger + "Same X. 40,000 years apart.") > three escalating mirrored beats > twist at about 80% > ending that loops into frame one.
Levers: recognition ("that's my uncle"), escalation, a running gag, recurring characters people root for, short series with cliffhangers.
Engines: Same Panic (events), Same Scam (shiny rock vs crypto), Same Group Chat, Same First Date, Same Boss, Same Diet Trend, Same Influencer.

## 7. Controversy
Heat drives comments, and comments drive reach. But some heat kills ads and invites strikes.
- Safe heat: dating and gender jabs, bosses, generational fights, scams and hype, influencers, diet trends, "which one are you".
- Avoid: politics, specific religions, race, real named people.
- Prophet mocks hype-men and doomsayers, never faith.
- Endings bait debate: "Which one are you?" or a deliberately arguable ranking.
